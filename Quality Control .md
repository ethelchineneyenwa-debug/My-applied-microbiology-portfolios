Project 4: Quality Control – Product Storage and Non-Conformance Management

Project Overview

This project demonstrates my understanding of basic Quality Control principles, including identifying quality problems, reporting non-conformances, investigating root causes, taking corrective action, and preventing recurrence.

Scenario

During a routine quality check in a food production environment, a product was discovered to have been stored at the wrong temperature.

Objective

To identify the cause of the storage problem, prevent potentially affected products from being released, and recommend corrective and preventive actions.

Quality Control Procedure

1. I would identify the affected product and report the issue to my supervisor or Quality Control Manager.
2. The affected product would be placed on hold while the problem was investigated.
3. I would check the storage equipment to determine whether it was functioning correctly.
4. I would review how and when the product was stored and check the storage temperature.
5. If the equipment was faulty, I would report the fault and ensure that it was repaired.
6. The affected product would be assessed according to the company’s quality and food-safety procedures.
7. If the product was confirmed to be unsafe or did not meet the required standard, it would be properly disposed of according to company procedures.
8. A new acceptable batch would be produced or supplied where necessary.

Identified Cause

The investigation identified faulty storage equipment as a possible cause of the incorrect storage temperature.

Corrective Action

The faulty equipment would be reported and repaired. The affected product would remain on hold until its quality and safety had been assessed.

Preventive Action

To prevent recurrence:

* Storage equipment should be checked before use.
* Temperature should be monitored and recorded regularly.
* Equipment should undergo routine maintenance.
* Staff should be properly trained on storage requirements.
* Any abnormal temperature reading should be reported immediately.

Documentation

The incident should be properly documented, including:

* The nature of the problem
* The affected product or batch
* Investigation findings
* Root cause
* Corrective action
* Preventive action
* Final disposition of the product

Skills Demonstrated

* Quality Control
* Non-conformance identification
* Root-cause investigation
* Corrective and preventive action (CAPA)
* Product handling and storage monitoring
* Documentation and record keeping
* Food safety awareness
* Problem-solving
* Team communication

Conclusion

This project demonstrates my ability to recognize a quality problem, report and investigate the issue, identify a possible root cause, recommend corrective action, and establish preventive measures to reduce the risk of recurrence.
